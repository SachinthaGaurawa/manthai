      Chapter I

      The Spirit Without Borders

      Our name honors Mantai, the ancient Indian Ocean crossroads where silk, craft, and distant cultures met without borders. House of MANTHAI carries that borderless spirit into contemporary Sri Lankan luxury womenswear: sculptural silhouettes, tactile fine fabrics, and disciplined tailoring designed to outlive every passing trend.

      Tailored Without Boundaries. Crafted to Endure.

      The Anatomy of Our Seal

      The Unbounded Spiral is an original sculptural emblem created exclusively for House of MANTHAI, uniting nature’s self-similar logarithmic growth with the fluid poise of haute couture.

      The Wordmark

      The Hidden Sun & Moon Cipher

      Drawn into three letters of our name is a quiet cipher of light. Most will never notice it. Those who do, carry it with them.

      1. The first AA rising solar horizon.
      2. The HA lunar crescent cradling a solar orb.
      3. The second AA waxing lunar crescent.

      Enduring as the Sun and Moon.
