      The Campaign

      THE MAISON — BORN WHERE EMPIRES TRADED BEAUTY

      Before modern luxury had a name, the world sailed to Mantai. Along the northwestern shores of ancient Ceylon stood Mahatittha—known to sixth-century chroniclers as the Great Emporium of the Indian Ocean, where Silk Road textiles, Mannar pearls, deep crimson garnets, and rare aromatics met royal craftsmanship.

      House of MANTHAI revives that sovereign spirit without borders. Rejecting conventional boundaries of fashion, every silhouette we conceive—from hand-painted wearable couture and sculpted tailoring to architectural leather goods and haute parfumerie—is created as a living heirloom. Beneath our heraldic Tree of Boundless Creation stand twin Ceylon leopards, rare guardians watching both East and West. And concealed quietly within the letterforms of our name lies the ancient royal vow of the Sun and Moon: a promise that true artistry is crafted to endure forever.

      1. The first A A rising solar horizon.
      2. The H A lunar crescent cradling a solar orb.
      3. The second A A waxing lunar crescent.

      Enduring as the Sun and Moon.
