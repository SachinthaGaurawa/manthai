      Tailored Without Boundaries. Crafted to Endure.

      Our name honors Mantai — the ancient Indian Ocean crossroads where silk, craft, and distant cultures met without borders. House of MANTHAI carries that borderless spirit into contemporary womenswear: sculptural silhouettes, hand-finished textiles, and architectural tailoring conceived far beyond seasonal trends.
